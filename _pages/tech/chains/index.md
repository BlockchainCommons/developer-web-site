_Blockchain Commons has worked with a variety of blockchains to
advance the [Gordian Principles](/principles/) and incorporate our
technologies_

***Why?*** Blockchains are where the rubber hits the road for digital
assets and (in some cases) digital identities. They're practical,
real-world examples of self-sovereign control of assets; the ultimate
goal of Blockchain Commons' specifications is real-world integration,
and blockchains are one of the prime opportunities for that.

## ![](/assets/badges/bitcoin.png) Bitcoin

**Cryptocurrency Blockchain.** Bitcoin remains the king of the
blockchains and was also our entry into the field, with
macro-architecture and our [Learning
Bitcoin](https://learningbitcoin.blockchaincommons.com/) course. Much
of our [technology](/technology/) such as [SSKR](/sskr/),
[LifeHash](/lifehash/), and [OIB](/oib/) was built with Bitcoin in
mind.

For more see:

* [**Bitcoin Blockchain**](/chains/bitcoin/)

## ![](/assets/badges/ethereum.png) Ethereum

**Smart Contract Cryptocurrency Blockchain.** The smart contracts of
Ethereum may be some of the smarest custody out there. We've long
considered ways to highlight this blockchain and hope to do so in the
near future.

## ![](/assets/badges/zcash.png) Zcash

**Privacy-Focused Cryptocurrency Blockchain.** We love the privacy
focus of Zcash because it supports our design philosophy of
[anti-coercion](/architecture/coercion-resistance/). We were thrilled
to add the [Gordian Principle](/principles/) of openness to their
stack with ZeWIF, an open wallet interchange format because on
[Gordian Envelope](/envelope/).

For more see:

* [**Zcash Blockchain**](/chains/zcash/)
