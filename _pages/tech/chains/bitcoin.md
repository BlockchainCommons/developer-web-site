_Bitcoin was the original cryptocurrency and therefore the focus of
much of Blockchain Commons' early works. What follows is a list of our
most Bitcoin-specific work and technologies & specifications that are
of particular use to Bitcoin developers._

## Bitcoin Projects

* [**Learning Bitcoin from the Command Line**](https://learningbitcoin.blockchaincommons.com/) - A hands-on introduction to Bitcoin.
* [**iOS Seedtool**](https://github.com/BlockchainCommons/GordianSeedTool-iOS) - Generate seeds, translate to keys, sign PSBTs.
* [**Smart Custody**](https://www.smartcustody.com/) - A course on managing digital assets, focused on Bitcoin.

## Bitcoin for Developers

* [**Animated QRs**](/animated-qrs/) - Transfer PSBTs across airgaps.
* [**CSR**](/csr/) - Store sharded shares of seeds on remote servers.
* [**Gordian Envelope**](/envelope/) - Store seeds, keys & descriptors with metadata.
* [**SSKR**](/sskr/) - Allow users to shard their seeds.
* [**UR**](/ur/) - Exchange data with other apps in an interoperable way.
