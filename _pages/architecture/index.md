_Blockchain Commons' technologies are built by a Trust Architect:
Christopher Allen. They are intended to fulfill the [Gordian
Principles](/principles/) as well as a variety of additional [design
philosophies](/design/) and [design
patterns](/architecture/patterns)._

## Gordian Principles

The Gordian Principles are, in short: independence, privacy,
resilience, and openness. They are intended to support
self-sovereignty (the ability for a user to control their own digital
destiny) and for it to be safe (because self-sovereignty can be scary
with no one to back you up).

The internet has become full of sharks who want to control your assets
and your identity. Self-sovereignty (and the Gordian principles that
it illumintes) ensures you won't be eaten alive.

* For more, see the [Gordian Principles](/principles/)

## Design Philosophies

Design Philosophies are big-picture ideas that are built into our work
to have specific effects. Though our
[specifications](https://github.com/BlockchainCommons/research/?tab=readme-ov-file)
are ultimately built to fulfill the [Gordian
Principles](/principles/), design philosophies offer the big-picture
strategies that we believe will fulfill those goals.

* For more, see the [Design Philosophies page](/architecture/design/)

## Design Patterns

If design philosophies are the big-picture, strategic ways to ensure
that specifications and applications meet the [Gordian
Principles](/principles/), then design patterns are the small-picture
tactics. We haven't fully laid out design patterns, but we've
described what they are are and also laid out some patterns for the
Heterogeneity philosophy.

* For more, see the [Design Patterns page](/architecture/patterns/)
* For examples, see [Authentication Design Patterns](/architecture/patterns/auth/)

## Macro-Architecture

_As Above, So Below._ Before we started developing specifications to
improve digital ecosystems, we focused on the Bitcoin ecosystem and
how a design philosophy of Heterogeneity (or Partition) could improve
its security. Many of our original reference apps worked toward this
goal. The architectural macro-design for the Bitcoin ecosystem remains
a strong foundation for services, and one we suggest our partners
adopt as they incorporate our specifications into their own
applications.

* For more, see the [Macro-architecture page](/architecture/macro/)
