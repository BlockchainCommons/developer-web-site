_Open Source is built on the desire to produce software that can be
openly used by the community. It's a great first step, But, Open
Source doesn't say that a project needs to be designed, developed, or
supported in an open way. The principle of Open Development offers
this expansion._

## The Principles of Open Development

Open Development is built on seven sub-principles:

1. **Accessibility.** Anyone must be able to join.
2. **Collaboration.** Members must have a voice in design.
3. **Diversity.** A diversity of views must be recruited.
4. **Strategy.** Plans must be made by the community as a whole.
5. **Transparency. ** Membership, goals, governance, processes, and support must be open.
6. **Sustainability.** Long-term commitment must be built in from the start.
7. **Openness.** Openness must also mean interoperability.

See ["Principles of Open
Development"](https://www.blockchaincommons.com/articles/Open-Development/#principles-of-open-development)
for more on these topics.

## The Levels of Open Development

Open Development isn't a binary choice, but instead a
spectrum. Projects can rise up through a variety of levels to
eventually meet the full requirements of Open Development.

0. **Open Source.** Source is mostly viewable and freely licensable.
1. **Inspectable.** Open source is available in a public repo.
2. **Observable.** Branches, releases, and issues are used to make development process open.
3. **Reproducible.** Code is compilable even if some parts remain private.
4. **Testable.** Open test suites support reproducible code.
5. **Cooperative.** Public is fully invited into design & development process.
6. **Distributed.** Project is shipped & in use by multiple entities.
7. **Standardized.** Project is accepted by international standards organization.

See ["Best Practices of Open
Development"](https://www.blockchaincommons.com/articles/Open-Development/#best-practices-of-open-development)
for more.
