The [Gordian Developer Meeting](https://www.blockchaincommons.com/subscribe/#gordian-developers) on **August 5, 2026** focused on how to better secure your Bitcoin with Smart Custody techniques such as multisig and Shamir's Secret Sharing. It included two feature presentations:

* **Sharding Secrets to Protect Your Bitcoin.** A discussion of the threat of "loss" to self-sovereign Bitcoin ownership, how that can be resolved with metadata and Shamir's Secret Sharing, and how Bitcoin Core can be brought into the process.
* **How Keylay Works.** A look at a novel technology that allows the secure transmission of QR codes across the internet.
